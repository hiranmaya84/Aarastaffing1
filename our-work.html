Candidate Quality

What We Screen For

Most agencies screen resumes. We screen the person — the things that actually predict field performance.

🗣️

Communication

Can this MR communicate confidently with GPs, specialists and hospital pharmacists? We assess this directly, not from a resume.

🗺️

Territory Knowledge

Does the candidate know the key doctors, chemists and stockists in their target HQ? Local knowledge is a significant head start.

📊

Stability Track Record

We review tenure patterns carefully. A candidate who has changed three jobs in two years is flagged — regardless of how good their resume looks.

💊

Product Background

Relevant division experience matters. An Ayurvedic MR placed in an Ethical Cardiology role is a mismatch we avoid.

🤝

Doctor Handling Ability

How does the candidate handle a busy specialist who gives them 2 minutes? Tested through scenario discussion during screening.

⚡

Availability & Urgency Match

Candidate availability, notice period and expected CTC are all verified before the shortlist reaches you — no surprises.
